Title: String of hearts
Subtitle: Metre-long trails full of tiny hearts.
Light: Bright, a bit of sun is fine
Watering: Sparingly, every two to three weeks - the leaves store water
Location: Somewhere high, so the trails can hang freely
Pets: Considered non-toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Yes, lay a piece of trail on moist soil
---
The string of hearts is the plant visitors always look at twice. Metre-long,
thin trails hang out of a small pot, covered in little heart-shaped leaves -
marbled grey-green on top, purple underneath.

It is actually a succulent, which makes it the opposite of demanding. The only
thing that really kills it is too much water. Better to water once too late
than once too often.

Cuttings couldn't be easier: I lay a piece of trail on a pot of moist soil, and
after a few weeks roots grow from the leaf nodes. Since the trails keep getting
too long anyway, I almost always have some to spare.

Title: Pothos
Subtitle: Grows everywhere, including where it shouldn't.
Light: Partial shade to bright, copes with dark corners too
Watering: Every 7 to 10 days, rather too little than too much
Cuttings: Yes, any time - a piece of vine in a glass of water is enough
---
The pothos is the plant for everyone who can't really do plants. It grows in
almost any light, forgives dry spells and turns every piece you cut off into a
new plant.

At my place it hangs down from the shelf and has conquered half the room by now.
That's exactly why I always have cuttings to spare.

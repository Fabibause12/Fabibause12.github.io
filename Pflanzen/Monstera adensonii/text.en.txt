Title: Monstera adansonii
Subtitle: The Monstera's little sister, with holes instead of slits.
Light: Bright, no harsh sun
Watering: Once a week, less often in winter
Location: Warm, ideally with a moss pole to climb
Pets: Mildly toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Yes, any time - a piece of vine with a node in a glass of water
---
The Monstera adansonii is also called Monkey Mask, and one look at the leaves
tells you why: every single one is full of oval holes, as if someone had turned
it into Swiss cheese. Mine climbs up a moss pole and has grown much wider than I
ever expected.

It grows fast and is pleasantly uncomplicated. When it gets too bushy, I cut off
a few shoots - just below a node, into a glass of water, and after two to three
weeks there are roots.

That's exactly why there are almost always cuttings of it. If you want one, just
let me know.

Title: Monstera
Subtitle: The Swiss cheese plant that makes every room look bigger.
Light: Bright, but no direct sunlight
Watering: Once a week, let the soil dry off a little first
Location: Room temperature, no draughts
Pets: Mildly toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Yes, as a stem cutting in a glass of water
---
The Monstera is the least complicated flatmate I have. She forgives you for
forgetting to water her for a week and only complains with drooping leaves -
after the next watering she stands tall again as if nothing had happened.

Cuttings are easy: I cut off a piece of stem just below an aerial root, put it in
a glass of water for two to three weeks, and as soon as the roots are two
centimetres long, it goes into normal potting soil.

If you want one, just let me know - there's plenty to go around.

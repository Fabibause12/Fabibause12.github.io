Title: Rubber plant
Subtitle: Glossy leaves, straight growth and hardly any demands.
Light: Bright, but better no harsh midday sun
Watering: Every one to two weeks, once the top of the soil is dry
Location: Warm, no draughts - and then just leave it there
Pets: The milky sap is toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Yes, as a tip cutting in a glass of water
---
I have two rubber plants: a small one on top of the shelf under the sloping
ceiling and a big one standing on the floor. Both do exactly what they're
supposed to: grow a bit taller every year and look good while doing it. The only thing it holds against you is moving - keep shifting it around
and it punishes you with a few yellow leaves.

The big leaves collect dust, so every now and then I wipe them with a damp
cloth. Afterwards they shine like new.

Cutting it releases a white milky sap. It's sticky and irritates the skin, so dab
it off or rinse it with lukewarm water. The cut-off tip roots in a glass of water
after a few weeks - if you want one, just let me know.

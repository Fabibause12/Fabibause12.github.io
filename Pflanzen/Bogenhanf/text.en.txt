Title: Snake plant
Subtitle: The plant for everyone who regularly forgets their plants.
Light: Bright to shady – takes almost any spot
Watering: Every two to three weeks, even less in winter
Location: Room temperature, not below 10 degrees
Pets: Mildly toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Yes, by dividing the rhizome or as a leaf cutting
---
The snake plant is the most undemanding plant I have. It still looks good in a
dark corner, needs hardly any water and just slowly grows along at its own pace.
It used to be called Sansevieria; botanically it now belongs to the dragon trees.

There is really only one way to kill it: too much water. If it stays wet for too
long, the rhizome rots away from below. Better a week too late than a day too
early.

There are two ways to propagate it: divide the rhizome when repotting, or cut a
leaf into pieces and let them dry off before they go into the soil. The second
way takes longer, but one leaf gives you several plants.

Title: Alocasia
Subtitle: Arrow-shaped leaves that look as if someone spilled white paint over them.
Light: Bright, but no direct sun
Watering: Keep the soil slightly moist, never waterlogged
Location: Warm and humid - mine lives in the bathroom
Pets: Toxic to cats and dogs
Cuttings: Rarely, from small corms when repotting
---
The Alocasia is the diva among my plants. Every leaf is patterned differently:
some are almost completely green, others nearly white, and most are marbled
somewhere in between.

It lives in my bathroom because it likes it warm and humid. Draughts, cold roots
or soggy soil are answered immediately with drooping or yellow leaves. When it's
happy, though, it pushes out one new leaf after another surprisingly fast.

The white parts of the leaves can't photosynthesise, so they turn brown sooner.
That's normal and not a care mistake. When repotting, small corms sometimes turn
up in the soil - then there's one to give away.

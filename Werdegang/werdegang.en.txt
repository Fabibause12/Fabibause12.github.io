# Introduction

## What I do professionally

Experimental physicist specialising in electron optics. I research the control of
free electrons with chip-based electrode structures – a topic that has been with
me since my bachelor's thesis. For me the path goes from simulation through the
layout on the chip and fabrication all the way to measurements in the electron
microscope. After that I would like to do a PhD in the field of semiconductor and
quantum devices.

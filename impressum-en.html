What the site stores

No cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics, no fonts from third-party servers. The browser remembers exactly two things: whether you chose the light or dark theme, and whether you want German or English. Both are kept in your own browser storage and never leave your device.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub B.V., Vijzelstraat 68-72, 1017 HL Amsterdam, Netherlands). When you visit it, your browser necessarily sends data to GitHub, including your IP address; I have no influence on that. What GitHub does with it is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The dice games also run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent and nothing is saved – reloading the page deletes every game.

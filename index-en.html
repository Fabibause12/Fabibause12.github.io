Research

Free electrons on chips

At the Chair of Laser Physics at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg (Prof. Peter Hommelhoff) I work on building blocks for a quantum electron microscope. Planar electrode structures create auto-ponderomotive potentials that guide, split and steer free electron beams above a chip.

In my master’s thesis (finishing end of 2026) I developed a beam switch and a ring resonator for multi-pass measurements – from FEM simulation of the electrode geometry through fabrication and bonding to the first measurement of a newly identified guiding regime.

Publication: “Planar On-Chip Auto-Ponderomotive Devices for Electron Beam Control”, IVNC 2024 (DOI). Everything else is listed under Career.

What this is

Hello friends – and those who want to become one

This is my private side project, where I play around with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. You’ll find the professional part under Career; the rest is for friends: links to people I like, my plants, the bees and a few dice games. Nothing to buy, no ads, no cookies.

Looking for a cutting? Under Plants you can see what is currently growing at my place and what you can have a piece of. Just let me know.

    About me

    I’m Fabian. As an experimental physicist, I get free electrons to fly over chips for a living, and I’m currently finishing my master’s. After graduating at the end of 2026 I’d like to do a PhD in semiconductor and quantum devices. On the side I keep bees, go bouldering and to the gym, and volunteer on trips for people with disabilities. The plants are just standing around in the way.

Title: Maria
Subtitle: A secondary swarm from April 2026 that grew to ten combs by summer.
Hive: Dadant, ten brood combs
Frames: 48 cm wide, 28.6 cm high
Moved in: 28 April 2026
Origin: Secondary swarm from Veronika, about 1.5 kg
Queen: First seen on 6 June 2026, golden-brown abdomen, otherwise black
Varroa: Lactic acid in May, formic acid in July and from 23 September
Stores: About 25 kg stored by the end of August 2026
---
Maria is a secondary swarm that I picked up from Veronika in Effeltrich at the end
of April 2026. Roughly one and a half kilos of bees, parked overnight in the
neighbour's garage, tipped out in front of the hive the next day. After a good
hour they were all inside – that moment when the whole heap starts moving and
marches up into the entrance is still my favourite part.

The bees live in a Dadant hive with two boxes, and the frames are added one by one
as soon as the colony can actually draw them out.
With Maria that went quickly: at the beginning of May the first two frames were a
third drawn, and by mid-May there was so much going on that three combs could go
in at once. By mid-June the tenth and last comb of the year was hanging in the box.

For a long time I didn't get to see the queen. Of course the brood pattern shows
that she's there – evenly capped areas, eggs on almost every comb. On 6 June she
was finally sitting on comb five: golden-brown abdomen, otherwise black. Since
then I find her again regularly.


I didn't harvest anything this year. A swarm gets to keep everything it brings in
during its first summer. By the end of August, with some extra feeding, about
25 kilos were stored, the brood nest looked good, and so she's ready for her
first winter.
